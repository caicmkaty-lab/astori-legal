Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Astori ("Astori," "the app," "we," "us," or "our") is a habit-tracking app developed by Chenmei Cai. We care about your privacy and designed Astori to need as little of your information as possible. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app does and does not handle, how it is used, and the choices and rights you have. It applies to the Astori app on iOS and Android.

By using Astori, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

Summary

  • Your habits and personal entries are stored only on your device. There is no account system and no server that stores your personal data, so we cannot see what you track.
  • The only information processed off your device relates to optional purchases (the Astori Pro subscription), which are handled by Apple, Google, and our subscription provider RevenueCat — never by us directly.
  • We do not use advertising, we do not run third-party analytics or tracking, we do not access your health data, and we never sell your personal information.

1. Information stored on your device

Everything you create in Astori — habit names, icons, colors, completion history, streaks, reminder times, themes, and app settings — is stored locally on your device using on-device storage. This information never leaves your device through us, is not uploaded to any server we operate, and is not accessible to us or shared with anyone. You control it directly in the app, and deleting the app removes it from your device.

2. Reminders and notifications

If you enable reminders, Astori schedules notifications locally on your device. They are generated and delivered on-device. We do not operate a notification server, and we collect no information about your reminders or whether you act on them.

3. Subscriptions and purchase information

Astori offers an optional paid subscription, "Astori Pro." Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive or store your name, payment card, or billing details.

To deliver Pro features and keep your subscription status accurate, we use RevenueCat, a third-party subscription-management service. In connection with a purchase or restore, RevenueCat processes a randomly generated identifier (not tied by us to your real-world identity); transaction details such as the product purchased, purchase and expiration dates, the store used, and store transaction identifiers; and limited technical metadata (such as platform, app version, and country) used to manage entitlements. This lets the app confirm whether your subscription is active. We do not link this information to your name or contact details.

The processing of your purchase by these providers is governed by their own privacy policies: Apple, Google, and RevenueCat.

4. Aggregate information from the app stores

When you download or use Astori, Apple and Google may provide us with aggregated, anonymized statistics through App Store Connect and Google Play Console — for example, total downloads, country-level breakdowns, and crash counts. This information is reported in aggregate and does not identify you personally. We use it only to understand overall app performance and stability.

5. Information we do not collect

To be explicit, Astori does not require an account, email, or sign-in; does not collect analytics about how you personally use the app; does not include advertising or advertising trackers; does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, precise location, or health/fitness data; and does not sell or share your personal information for advertising.

6. How information is used

The limited information described above is used only to operate the app's core features on your device, to verify and restore your Astori Pro subscription, and to understand aggregate, anonymized app performance. We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

7. Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on performance of a contract (to provide the app and your subscription) and our legitimate interests (to keep the app working and stable), in each case limited to the minimal data described here. Where required, we rely on consent, which you may withdraw at any time.

8. Data retention

Your on-device data remains on your device until you delete it or remove the app. Purchase-related data held by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat is retained under their respective policies for as long as needed to manage subscriptions and meet legal obligations.

9. Security

Because your personal habit data stays on your device, its security is tied to your device's own protections (such as your passcode and device encryption). For the limited subscription data, we rely on the security measures of Apple, Google, and RevenueCat. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. International data transfers

Apple, Google, and RevenueCat may process the limited subscription data described above on servers in the United States or other countries, which may have different data-protection laws than your own. By using the app's purchase features, you understand that this data may be processed outside your country of residence under those providers' policies and safeguards.

11. Children's privacy

Astori is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we do not collect personal information through the app in the first place, this risk is minimal. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us at astori.theapp@gmail.com.

12. Your privacy rights

You are always in control of your on-device data: you can view, edit, or delete it in the app, or remove it entirely by deleting the app.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights regarding personal data. The main personal data processed off your device is the subscription information held by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat under their own policies; you can also contact us with any request and we will help where we can.

  • EEA/UK (GDPR): you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data, and to data portability. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.
  • California (CCPA/CPRA): you may have the right to know what personal information is collected, to access or delete it, to correct it, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.
  • Canada (PIPEDA): you may have the right to access your personal information and to challenge its accuracy.

To exercise any right, email us at astori.theapp@gmail.com. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law, and may need to confirm your request in relation to the limited data we can access.

13. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

Astori does not track you across other apps or websites, so there is no cross-context tracking to signal. We honor applicable opt-out preferences by simply not engaging in tracking.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above, and significant changes will be reflected in the app or on this page. Your continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

15. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your privacy, email us at astori.theapp@gmail.com.
